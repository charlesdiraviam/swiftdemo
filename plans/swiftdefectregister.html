The jump bar now overflows badly on mobile

MeasuredAt 375px the pill rail needs scrollWidth 1026 inside clientWidth 349 — roughly seven of eleven pills sit off-screen behind a horizontal scroll.

CauseFixing N5 (nothing hidden on mobile) and N4 (two pills added) took the bar from 4 visible pills to 11 competing for the same 349px. The old build hid the overflow; this one scrolls it.

SuggestionEleven destinations is more than a thumb bar can carry. Cut to the 4–5 that matter on mobile (Triage · Wait · Check-in · Location · Pricing) and let the header menu own the rest — or make the bar a single "Jump to…" sheet.
